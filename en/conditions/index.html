1. Purpose

Backspace is an Android application that helps you free up space on your phone by going through your photo library and suggesting what you could delete. These terms govern your use of the app.

By installing Backspace, you accept them.

2. What the app does, and what it never decides on its own

Backspace suggests. You decide.

No item is moved to the trash without you confirming a screen that names the number of items and their destination, and then the Android system confirmation dialog. There is no automatic deletion in the app, no scheduled deletion, and no shortcut that would bypass that confirmation.

Confirmed items are moved to the system trash, where they stay recoverable for 30 days. Backspace has no means of permanently deleting a file.

The app is free and unlimited in use: there is no cap on the number of items you may review or delete, neither per session nor per day.
